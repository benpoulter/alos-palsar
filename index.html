Reading the numbers. Selections marked 100 m are counted pixel by pixel from the original maps; those marked 2 km use a summary grid (regional totals within 0.1%, single cells ±2%). The radar misses roughly 40% of flooded forest under dense canopy (South America validation), so treat areas as minimums. Central Africa has not yet been validated separately.

Trend is the Theil–Sen slope of yearly means, with a Mann–Kendall test (p < 0.05 means the direction is unlikely to be chance). Gap-filled is the share of flooded area that was filled in from neighbouring dates or a hydrological proxy rather than seen directly.
